8. Security
    
    **Headline:** 
    
    Control what touches your systems. See everything it does.
    
    **Description:**
    
    Writing to a live CMMS or ERP is where teams get nervous, and they are right to be. So Makini gives you the controls, not just the connection. You decide what each integration can touch, you see everything it does, and you can shut it off in a click. Built to satisfy the most demanding security teams in industry.
    
    **Pillars:**
    
    1. Scoped, revocable access
        
        Each connection runs on its own token, scoped to what its credentials can do in the source system. Revoke it and access is cut off immediately.
        
    2. A log of every action
        
        Every call is logged and searchable, from API requests to workflow executions. Stream the audit trail into Sentry, a webhook, or syslog, so it sits where your team already looks
        
    3. Independently verified
        
        SOC 2 Type II, UK Cyber Essentials, and GDPR compliant with independent tests twice a year.
        
    4. Your data, your region
        
        Keep data in-region, or on your own infrastructure. In-country hosting for the EU, UK, and Australia, and self-hosting available on request. GovCloud and ITAR are supported and running in production today
