Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Makini Trust Center

How do I request trust-center access?

Click “Request access” and fill in your name, email, company, reason, and the specific documents you need. You can choose full or limited access depending on your role — existing customers and prospective customers are both welcome.

Which resources are directly downloadable?

The SOC 2 Type 2 Report and the bulk archive of all policies are available for immediate download — no approval needed. All other documents require submitting an access request.

How many security controls does Makini maintain?

Makini maintains 36 passing controls across five domains: Infrastructure security, Organizational security, Product security, Internal security procedures, and Data and privacy.
